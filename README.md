# Meridian Investment Workbench

React + TypeScript dashboards backed by a Python/FastAPI investment pipeline. The application runs locally with persistent SQLite state. The Equities page uses Alpaca prices and SEC annual reports; the FX page uses Yahoo Finance via yfinance with Twelve Data fallback. Bonds, Rates, Commodities and the simulated portfolio use an explicit synthetic provider. The demo workspace works without API keys.

## Live Equities and research tools

Open **http://127.0.0.1:8000/markets/equities**. The server authenticates with Alpaca; credentials never enter the React bundle or browser storage. The paper trading host supplies the market clock; quotes, snapshots and historical bars come from `https://data.alpaca.markets/v2`. This integration sends GET requests only and never places Alpaca orders or changes the simulated portfolio.

On this workstation, credentials are stored in a Windows DPAPI blob at `../.runtime/secrets/alpaca.dpapi`, encrypted for the current Windows user and excluded from the release archive. For another machine or account, set backend environment variables `ALPACA_API_KEY` and `ALPACA_SECRET_KEY` before starting the server. No frontend `VITE_` credential variables are used. Set `ALPACA_ENABLED=false` to disable the connector. Rotated environment credentials override the local encrypted blob.

The screener searches the full active US-equity directory from Alpaca `GET /v2/assets` by ticker or company name. Search includes listings beyond the 12 initial symbols, ranks exact/prefix ticker matches first, and paginates all matches. Select a result to fetch its real quotes and adjusted history and open its research workbench. The directory is cached for one hour; errors are shown explicitly. Compare up to 25 symbols at once, removing rows or replacing the selected symbol when full. This comparison limit does not limit searchable listings. Directory membership does not guarantee IEX quote/history coverage; broker tradability is displayed separately. It polls every 30 seconds while visible; snapshots/clock are cached for 20 seconds and history for 15 minutes. The IEX feed covers one exchange, not consolidated SIP/NBBO. It shows market-open/closed state and actual trade timestamps; closed-market observations are latest-session prices. Failures appear explicitly and do not fall back to synthetic observations. Charts and momentum use completed, corporate-action-adjusted daily bars; the latest trade is an unadjusted quote. Recent incomplete daily bars are excluded.

- **Financial strength:** all nine Piotroski F-Score tests, pass/fail/unknown coverage, book-to-market and earnings yield. Enter comparable current/prior annual statements with fiscal dates, filing dates and a source. Accounting amounts are USD millions; share counts are millions and must be split-consistent with the current price. Prior beginning assets require the preceding balance. SEC EDGAR supplies standard annual facts for the selected company when available; your saved manual worksheet takes precedence. Missing or ambiguous inputs remain unknown and a total F-Score appears only when all nine tests are available. Banks and insurers require sector-specific interpretation.
- **Moat and valuation:** historical ROIC versus assumed WACC, editable forward ROIC/capital growth/tax/competitive-advantage duration, a fair-value sensitivity table and market-implied advantage period. This is a simplified economic-profit scenario: invested capital plus discounted excess returns during the advantage period, zero excess returns thereafter, then a net-debt bridge to equity. The implied duration is conditional on those assumptions and may have no solution in the 0-50-year search. Qualitative competitive-advantage notes are saved in this browser only.
- **Value and momentum:** completed-bar 12-1 and 6-1 price momentum, 63-session annualized volatility, and equal-weight book-to-market/momentum percentile ranks across at least three stocks with complete inputs. ETFs are excluded from stock ranks. This small watchlist comparison is not a replication of the published cross-market portfolios or an expected-return forecast.
- **Implementation:** editable scenario holdings/NAV/target, an absolute-weight no-trade buffer, rounded shares, turnover, IEX spread, assumed fees/impact, optional realized-gain tax and a one-way cost hurdle. IEX-only volume is not consolidated trading capacity. This calculator is independent of Alpaca account positions and the demo book, and submits no orders.

Financial worksheets and SEC import metadata are stored separately in SQLite with audit events; worksheets and per-field source provenance can be exported. Sources for all five papers and the limits of each translation are linked on the page. Broader custom-tag/IFRS coverage, a full DCF, calibrated impact models and production factor backtests require further data adapters and validation.

New backend modules: `alpaca.py` (read-only provider/cache), `credentials.py` (server-only credential loading), `equity_analysis.py` (statement scoring and scenario calculations), and `sec.py` (EDGAR reports and annual fact mapping). Endpoints: `GET /api/equities/search?q=...&offset=0&limit=25`, `GET /api/equities/market`, `GET/PUT /api/equities/financials/{symbol}`, `POST /api/equities/{symbol}/valuation` and `POST /api/equities/{symbol}/implementation`.

## SEC 10-K connection

Selecting a company in the equities screener automatically requests `POST /api/equities/sec/{symbol}/sync`. The backend resolves an exact SEC ticker/CIK mapping, retrieves the submissions history and company-facts JSON, selects the latest original Form 10-K by fiscal period, downloads the full filing, and imports matching standard US-GAAP annual facts. The workbench shows the fiscal period, filing date, CIK, accession, original SEC link, full report download, warnings, and per-field tag/formula/source provenance. A manual reload refreshes the report; normal selections use a one-hour report cache and the ticker mapping is cached for a day.

The APIs are public and do not require an SEC API key. Requests identify this application, are serialized at no more than four per second, and never send Alpaca credentials. Set backend `SEC_USER_AGENT` to your application name and real administrator contact for your deployment, following SEC fair-access guidance; the default identifies Meridian's single-user research client without inventing a contact address. Set `SEC_ENABLED=false` to disable external SEC access. Isolated test instances disable real requests by default.

Annual facts are matched on accession, end date, USD/share units and 330-380-day duration; fiscal-year labels alone are insufficient because comparative figures share the latest filing's FY label. Latest-report comparatives take precedence; older original 10-Ks can provide opening balances. Ambiguous values and missing tags remain blank. Gross profit can be derived from reported revenue minus cost of revenue. Total debt is derived only when the current and noncurrent debt components, or total long-term debt plus explicit short-term borrowings, are both available. Invested capital uses book equity plus debt minus cash only when every component is available and the result is positive. Absent short-term debt and issuance tags are never assumed zero. Zero cash issuance cannot establish absence of non-cash issuance, so the no-issuance F-Score test often requires manual review.

The API does not supply custom dimensional tags through company facts. Foreign issuers that file 20-F/40-F and funds without a company 10-K are explicitly marked unavailable for this importer. A 10-K amendment is linked and flagged for review; this importer uses the original annual report. Annual shares remain historical and require review for subsequent splits and share-class differences. WACC, forecast ROIC, growth and competitive-advantage duration remain user assumptions.

Imported SEC data is stored under a separate `equity_sec` key. Existing manual worksheets and unsaved browser edits are preserved; edited worksheet values apply after Save & calculate. Repeated cached sync requests are idempotent. No portfolio cash, holdings, rebalancing or Alpaca orders are changed. SEC failures appear in the panel, preserve saved inputs and offer a retry. A filing download failure is distinguished from available financial facts.

Full filings are cached in `data/sec-cache/`, excluded from the release archive, and served as attachments from `GET /api/equities/sec/{symbol}/document` with a sandbox CSP. Filing HTML is not injected into the React page or interpreted as instructions. SEC responses, including embedded filing text, are external data.

References: [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), [EDGAR access and fair-access guidance](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data).

### Yahoo/yfinance primary and automatic fallback

Yahoo sometimes reports a valid closing price outside its own high/low range. The closing-price chart validates each source close independently, preserving it exactly. Missing or inconsistent OHLC ranges become null open/high/low fields with `ohlc_available=false`; the chart footer counts affected observations in the displayed window. Invalid closes, wrong symbol/currency/type, conflicting timestamps and future timestamps still reject the Yahoo series and select a whole Twelve Data response if available. `FX_PRIMARY_PROVIDER=yahoo` is the default. Set `FX_PRIMARY_PROVIDER=twelvedata` to reverse the order; Yahoo then becomes the backup. `YAHOO_FX_ENABLED=false` disables Yahoo requests. Invalid daily closes are held for six hours before retrying; other pair-specific failures are held for five minutes. A healthy primary does not query Twelve Data usage or consume its rate/history credits. Missing credentials are diagnosed before any credit reservation. Yahoo does not always supply market state. Other unknown states remain unknown. Quotes older than 15 minutes during an unconfirmed/open session trigger backup lookup; source timestamps remain visible. The pinned yfinance client and its dependencies install into a separate `../.runtime/yfinance` directory using `backend/requirements-yahoo.lock.txt`. ## Rebalancing on Portfolio & Risk

The rebalancing workspace sits below **Your book**. Set per-instrument target weights with sliders or number inputs; cash is the residual. Use the transition-strength slider to compare a partial move with the full target. Optional instruments can be added from the paper-eligible universe. The backend rounds native lots, simulates sells before buys, deducts adverse slippage and fees, and recomputes the same four risk metrics using the projected post-cost holdings. The comparison includes before/after percentages, dollar VaR/ES, turnover, costs, cash, a historical index chart and an exportable trade basket. Invalid or unfunded scenarios cannot execute.

Save the draft targets as a strategic policy to anchor drift monitoring. The recommendation checks each validated snapshot against those saved targets, holds inside tolerance bands, and makes partial corrections toward their nearest boundaries outside the bands. A turnover budget scales the normal recommendation; immaterial weight changes are suppressed. Hard risk breaches take priority. Defaults of ±2 percentage points, 50% correction and 10% gross traded notional/NAV per review are illustrative settings to calibrate in walk-forward tests, not paper-prescribed optimal values. Manual scenarios may exceed this soft turnover budget; the interface flags the override. All concentration, gross exposure, cash and tradability limits remain hard gates.

The UI refreshes workspace data every 30 seconds while visible and on explicit refresh, without overwriting draft sliders. It does not ingest new external feeds itself. The future data adapters must publish fresh, validated snapshots with correct quote units, vintages, lot sizes and historical coverage. Reliable prices alone do not supply expected returns, signal persistence, transaction-cost/impact parameters or executable liquidity. Recommendation outputs remain conditional on the saved policy and available models.

**Approve paper rebalance** executes the reviewed basket only. It rejects changed holdings, quotes, policies or limits, rechecks the book and all trades, and commits cash, quantities, costs and hash-linked fill events in one database transaction. Failed baskets roll back completely. Retrying an identical request does not trade twice. No unattended rebalancing is scheduled.

Research translation: Boyd et al. motivate explicit constraints/costs; Donohue–Yip motivate no-trade bands and boundary corrections; Gârleanu–Pedersen motivate partial adjustment; Almgren–Chriss motivate separating allocation from execution urgency. This is an explainable policy heuristic, not a numerical replication of those papers. A multi-period optimizer, predictive aim portfolio and impact-optimal schedule require calibrated forecasts and liquidity inputs. The 2003 publisher text was inaccessible; its role is based on the supplied summary, with the no-trade-region principle also supported by Leland's public research. References and these limits are exposed in the recommendation panel.

API routes: `GET/PUT /api/rebalance/policy`, `POST /api/rebalance/preview`, and `POST /api/rebalance/execute`.

## Backend modules

Portfolio & Risk includes historical 1-day VaR and expected shortfall (95%/99%), maximum/latest drawdown, and annualized tracking error. Choose 63/126/252 aligned daily observations and a 60/40 SPX/IG, SPX or IG benchmark. The backend applies today's marked portfolio weights to historical price returns with zero-return cash. Drawdowns refer to this hypothetical daily-rebalanced portfolio, not actual account NAV history. ES uses fractional probability mass at the tail boundary; tracking error uses sample daily active-return standard deviation × sqrt(252). ## Scope and limitations

- No LLM provider is connected. Specialist outputs are clearly identified as rule-based; natural-language questions are saved as context, with exact symbol filtering. Open-ended semantic research requires a future LLM adapter.
- Paper execution supports selected USD equities and illustrative Treasury cash bonds only. FX, rate series, indices and commodities are monitoring-only.
- Bond quantities are face amount and valuation is face × price / 100. DV01 is a duration approximation. Coupon/accrual, settlement, corporate actions, funding, borrow, FX conversion, margin and contract roll/delivery are not implemented.
- Backtests are price-return experiments on synthetic weekday bars, with full fills. They omit exchange holidays and the preceding lifecycle cash flows. They are not evidence of strategy performance.
- This is a local single-user application. It binds to loopback, rejects cross-origin writes, and has no production identity integration. Add authentication, authorization, TLS, secret management and operational monitoring before exposing it to other users or connecting sensitive data.
- Hash linking detects altered records during verification; it does not make storage immutable against a privileged operator rewriting the database.
